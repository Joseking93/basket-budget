Where the money went

Spending over time

Monthly report

Budget

From your wealth tracker, against the month selected above. Edit any figure. Categories a budget doesn't claim are counted separately — a budget you meet by not knowing where money went isn't worth much.

Track a budget line

One line, every month — so you can see whether groceries are creeping up rather than only how last month compared with the one before. Counts the same way the budget does: splits land on each category, and a payment you assigned to another month counts there.

Refunds

Money coming back. Point a refund at the payment it repays and it reduces that cost instead of counting as income — 3,000 paid and 2,500 returned cost you 500, and that's the only figure that's true.

Shopping receipts

A statement line says what went to the supermarket. It cannot say what that bought. Receipts scanned in Basket appear here by themselves, each matched to the payment that settled it — so a grocery total becomes a list you can search. The box below is for a list copied from another device.

Recurring payments

Payments that repeat on a schedule — what you're committed to before deciding anything. Also where forgotten spending hides.

Balance over time
Closing balance each day, straight from the statements.

Fuliza & borrowing

What I owe

Balances from your wealth tracker, kept current from the imported statements. Revolving lines (Zenka, M-Shwari, KCB) can go up as well as down — repaying isn't the same as clearing. Items nothing can match are marked; record those payments by hand.

Needs categorising

Nothing is guessed here — these are names the app has no rule for, biggest first. Pick a category and it becomes a permanent rule for that name, applied to every past and future transaction. Work down from the top; the first few are usually most of the money.

Account check — is the same money counted twice?

Money moving between your bank and M-PESA appears in both statements. Each pair below was matched, so it is counted once, not twice.

Biggest merchants

People & name rules

Type a name and pick where it belongs — every transaction containing that name follows, past and future. Use a surname or first name; M-PESA writes the same person's number differently in an SMS and a statement, but the name stays put. Tag someone Money Lent to see what they still owe you.

Categories

Add your own — Gas, Pets, School fees, whatever you track. They appear in every dropdown straight away.

Settings — who you are

Your own numbers, kept in this browser and never in the app's code. The app needs them to tell a top-up to your own wallet apart from a payment to someone else — without them, money moving between your accounts gets counted twice.

People you know

A full number here also names them in bank rows, which only ever show digits.

Data & sync

Everything lives in this browser's storage — this device only. Clearing browser data wipes it, so back up now and then.

Moving between phone and laptop

Send a backup from one device and restore it on the other. Everything is keyed by M-PESA receipt, so importing can't double-count and re-importing the same file does nothing. Do it both ways and both devices end up with everything.

Transactions, rules and categories merge. Single values — a budget amount, a debt balance — take the incoming file's version, so restoring the older device into the newer one can overwrite a figure you just fixed. It reports what changed.
